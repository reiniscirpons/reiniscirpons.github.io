    About

    I'm Reinis, a PhD candidate in pure mathematics at the University of St Andrews. I work within the Algebra & Combinatorics research group under the supervision of professor James D. Mitchell. My research is focused on computational semigroup theory.

    My current research efforts involve:

    • Investigating minimum degree transformation representations of semigroups via their congruence lattice.
    • Applying methods from automata theory to aid the design of algorithms for solving the word problem in various classes of semigroups such as bands and inverse semigroups.
    • Solving the word problem in particular instances of one-relation monoids in search of small presentations whose decidability is unknown with current methods.

    See Publications, Software and Talks sections of this website for a more detailed overview of what I've been up to recently.
